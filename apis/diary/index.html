What actually happened

  1. The Gateway's frontend TLS validation uses mode: AllowInsecureFallback. The TLS handshake succeeds even without a valid client certificate.
  2. A TrafficPolicy on the https listener only allows requests where connection.peer_certificate_valid is true. RBAC returns a 403 for the rest.
  3. A ListenerPolicy local-reply mapper turns that 403 into a 302 to this page, adding ?reason=….
